## AGProLabs — clearer market context

[AGProLabs](https://www.agprolabs.com/) brings together rule-based chart analysis and candidate-review workflows. I focus on readable interfaces, explicit data states and documentation that separates an observation from a decision.

The [collections](https://www.agprolabs.com/collections/) organize tools by purpose. The [Insights library](https://www.agprolabs.com/insights/) explains concepts, usage questions and limitations before you decide whether a product fits your research.

### Engineering principles I care about

- **State before style:** missing, provisional, stale and confirmed information should remain distinguishable.
- **Time matters:** a reference's position on a historical chart and the time it became knowable are separate questions.
- **Units matter:** scores, distances and historical sample percentages answer different questions.
- **Evidence before claims:** compilation, runtime behavior, profiling and real-world outcomes need different forms of validation.
- **Readable workflows:** a panel should help the reader understand the next research step without implying certainty.

These are design priorities, not a claim that every product has achieved a particular performance benchmark. Market tools do not guarantee outcomes or replace independent risk and execution review. This repository introduces projects and resources; it does not distribute private or invite-only Pine source code.
